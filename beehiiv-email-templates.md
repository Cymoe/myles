## How to Add These in Beehiiv:

1. Go to your automation
2. For each email:
   - Click "Add Step" → "Wait" → Set days
   - Click "Add Step" → "Send Email"
   - Copy/paste subject and body
   - Save

3. Test with your own email first!

## Notes:
- Keep formatting simple (Beehiiv will handle styling)
- The [LINK] placeholder - add your actual sales page URL
