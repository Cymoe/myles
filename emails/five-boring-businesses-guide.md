## Business #1: Mobile RV Repair

**The Opportunity:**
- RV ownership exploded post-2020 (10M+ RVs on the road)
- Dealers are booked 3-6 months out
- Mobile techs charge $125-200/hour + parts

**Real Numbers:**
- Startup cost: $15K (van + tools)
- Revenue potential: $500K-1.5M/year
- Net margins: 40-50%
- Hours: You choose (most work weekends when people use RVs)

**Why It's Undervalued:**
- No sexy tech angle
- Requires getting hands dirty
- Most people don't know RVs exist

**Finding Sellers:**
Search "RV repair" on Google Maps. Call everyone with under 10 reviews.

## Business #2: Commercial Hood Cleaning

**The Opportunity:**
- Every restaurant needs it (fire code requirement)
- Recurring revenue (monthly/quarterly service)
- Work nights = no competition for employee attention

**Real Numbers:**
- Average job: $800-2,000
- 20 regular clients = $200K/year
- 50 clients = $600K/year
- Net margins: 55-65%

**Why It's Undervalued:**
- Dirty work (literally)
- Night shifts scare buyers
- Looks like a "job" not a business

**Finding Sellers:**
Restaurant supply companies know everyone. Buy them coffee.

## Business #3: Parking Lot Striping

**The Opportunity:**
- Every parking lot needs restriping every 2-3 years
- One truck can do $1M+ annually
- Commercial property managers need you

**Real Numbers:**
- Equipment cost: $25K
- Charge: $0.20-0.40 per linear foot
- Walmart lot: $15-25K
- Completion time: 1 night
- Net margins: 60-70%

**Why It's Undervalued:**
- No one dreams of painting lines
- Seasonal in cold climates
- Requires night work

**Finding Sellers:**
Join property management associations. They all know who's good.

## Business #4: Commercial Laundry Routes

**The Opportunity:**
- Hotels, restaurants, gyms need linen service
- Contracts = predictable revenue
- Route density = higher margins

**Real Numbers:**
- Routes sell for 1-1.5x annual revenue
- Established route: $500K-2M revenue
- Net margins: 25-35%
- Work hours: 4am-noon (done by lunch)

**Why It's Undervalued:**
- Early mornings
- Sounds unglamorous
- Industrial washing = scary to newbies

**Finding Sellers:**
Linen suppliers want to exit routes. Check industrial real estate brokers.

## Business #5: Roll-Off Dumpster Rental

**The Opportunity:**
- Construction boom = dumpster demand
- $400-800 per rental
- 30 dumpsters = $300K+/year
- Repeat customers (contractors)

**Real Numbers:**
- Truck + 10 containers: $150K
- Revenue per container/month: $1,200-2,000
- Scale to 50+ containers: $1M+ revenue
- Net margins: 35-45%

**Why It's Undervalued:**
- "Garbage business" stigma
- Heavy equipment scares people
- Requires CDL drivers

**Finding Sellers:**
Every waste management company has small operators wanting out.

## The Magic Formula

**What makes these businesses goldmines:**

1. **Recurring Revenue** - Customers need you monthly/yearly
2. **High Margins** - 40-70% net common
3. **Essential Services** - Not optional luxuries
4. **Fragmented Markets** - No dominant players
5. **Baby Boomer Exits** - Owners aging out
