# MK Deal Flow Newsletter Template Instructions

## Overview
This template is designed for your regular deal flow newsletters sent via Beehiiv. It follows the successful format of SMB Deal Hunter while maintaining your MK brand identity.

## Template Structure

### 1. **Subject Lines**
Use specific, value-driven subject lines that highlight the best deal:
- ✅ "Texas roofing company - $1.8M EBITDA at 3.2x"
- ✅ "5 new deals: HVAC company with 60% recurring revenue"
- ✅ "Phoenix plumbing biz - $850K profit, owner financing"
- ❌ "Your weekly deal update" (too generic)
- ❌ "Great opportunities inside" (no specifics)

### 2. **Opening**
- Keep it brief and energetic
- Tell them exactly how many deals you're sharing
- Build anticipation for the best one

### 3. **Community Top Picks** (Optional)
- Shows social proof and engagement
- List 3 deals that got the most interest from last issue
- Creates FOMO for readers who missed them

### 4. **New Deals Section**
For each deal, include:
- **Number & Business Type** (e.g., "1/ Texas Roofing Company")
- **Key Metrics** with emojis for visual appeal:
  - 📍 Location
  - 💰 Asking Price
  - 💼 EBITDA
  - 📊 Revenue
  - 📅 Year Established
- **Your Analysis** ("My 2 Cents"):
  - Why it's interesting
  - Key strengths
  - Main risks
  - Deal structure thoughts
  - Growth opportunities
- **View Listing Link** (if available)

### 5. **Community Wins**
- Share success stories from buyers
- Creates social proof
- Shows your network is getting results

### 6. **Call to Action**
- Clear offer for 1-on-1 advisory
- Positioned as helping them close deals
- Strong contrasting button

### 7. **Resources**
- Link to your free tools
- Reinforces value proposition
- Helps new subscribers catch up

### 8. **Sign Off**
- Personal and friendly
- P.S. encourages replies and engagement

## Best Practices

### Deal Selection
- **Quality over quantity**: 3-5 great deals better than 10 mediocre ones
- **Variety**: Mix industries, sizes, and geographies
- **Realistic**: Include deals your audience can actually afford/finance

### Writing Your Analysis
- **Be specific**: "34% EBITDA margins" not "good margins"
- **Show expertise**: Point out things buyers might miss
- **Be honest**: Call out real risks, don't sugarcoat
- **Actionable**: Suggest negotiation strategies

### Frequency
- **Consistency is key**: Same day/time each week
- **Recommended**: Tuesday or Thursday mornings
- **Avoid**: Mondays (busy) and Fridays (weekend mode)

### Engagement Tips
- **Ask questions**: "What industries are you looking at?"
- **Encourage replies**: "Seen a good deal? Send it over"
- **Track metrics**: Which deals get clicked most?

## How to Use in Beehiiv

1. **Create Campaign**: New broadcast campaign
2. **Copy HTML**: Use the template HTML provided
3. **Customize Content**: Replace placeholder text with real deals
4. **Preview**: Test on mobile and desktop
5. **Schedule**: Set for optimal send time
6. **Track Performance**: Monitor open rates, clicks, replies

## Subject Line A/B Testing Ideas
Test different approaches:
- Deal-specific: "Dallas HVAC - $850K profit, seller financing"
- Number-focused: "5 businesses under $5M (all cash flowing)"
- Urgency: "Manufacturing deal closes Friday - $2.1M EBITDA"
- Question: "Want a business doing $5M revenue?"

## Metrics to Track
- **Open rate**: Target 30-40%
- **Click rate**: Target 15-25%
- **Reply rate**: Target 2-5%
- **Advisory inquiries**: Track source

## Remember
- This is a relationship business - be helpful, not salesy
- Your analysis and insights are the real value
- Consistency builds trust and anticipation
- Every email should deliver value, even if they don't buy
