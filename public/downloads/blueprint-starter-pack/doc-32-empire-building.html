The 10-Year Wealth Building Plan

Phase 1: Foundation (Years 1-2)

  • Target: $1-2M revenue service business
  • Focus: Systems and processes
  • Goal: 2x EBITDA in 18 months
  • Exit: Keep or sell for 3-4x profit

Key Milestones:

  • Month 6: Profitable and stable
  • Month 12: Management in place
  • Month 18: Ready to scale
  • Month 24: Second acquisition

Phase 2: Expansion (Years 3-4)

Portfolio Building

  • Add 1-2 businesses per year
  • Mix of industries for stability
  • Geographic concentration for efficiency
  • Combined revenue: $8-12M
  • Central office established

Infrastructure Development:

  • Hire portfolio CFO
  • Centralized accounting
  • Management development program
  • Acquisition team formed

Phase 3: Consolidation (Years 5-7)

Platform Creation

  • Focus on 1-2 best industries
  • Roll-up strategy execution
  • 15-20 locations/businesses
  • $25-40M combined revenue
  • Professional board established

Phase 4: Empire (Years 8-10+)

Institutional Scale

  • $50-100M+ revenue platform
  • Multiple exit options
  • Generational wealth created
  • Legacy business built
