Common Automation Mistakes

What NOT to Do

  1. Over-automating too fast: Start small, prove ROI
  2. Losing personal touch: Keep human available
  3. Poor data hygiene: Garbage in = garbage out
  4. No training plan: Staff must adopt tools
  5. Ignoring integration: Systems must talk
  6. Set and forget: Regular optimization needed
