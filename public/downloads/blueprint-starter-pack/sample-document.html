The Hidden Truth About Service Businesses

While everyone's chasing tech startups and crypto, there's a boring goldmine hiding in plain sight: local service businesses that print money.

I'm talking about painting companies, HVAC services, ADU builders - the unsexy businesses that actually generate cash flow from day one.

The Formula: Buy, Optimize, Stack

Step 1: Buy Undervalued

  • Target 2-3x cash flow (most pay 5-7x)
  • Look for owner burnout signals
  • Focus on businesses with obvious fixes
  • Never pay more than you can finance

Step 2: Optimize Ruthlessly

  • Install remote management systems
  • Hire operators, not employees
  • Cut waste, not quality
  • Double prices within 90 days (yes, really)

Step 3: Stack Relentlessly

  • Buy complementary businesses
  • Cross-sell between companies
  • Share overhead and systems
  • Exit as a portfolio for 10x
