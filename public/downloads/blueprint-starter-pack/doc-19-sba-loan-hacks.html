Hack #1: The Experience Workaround

No Industry Experience? No Problem

Option A: The Key Employee Strategy

  • Identify experienced manager in the business
  • Offer 10-20% equity stake
  • Their experience counts for SBA
  • Structure with buy-back option

Option B: The Consultant Play

  • Hire industry consultant pre-close
  • 6-month contract minimum
  • Include in loan application
  • Counts as "management expertise"

Option C: The Adjacent Experience Angle

  • Management experience in ANY business
  • P&L responsibility counts
  • Emphasize transferable skills
  • Create detailed transition plan

Hack #2: The Down Payment Engineering

Creative Down Payment Structure

Purchase Price: $1,000,000 Traditional Structure: - SBA Loan: $900,000 (90%) - Your Cash: $100,000 (10%) Engineered Structure: - SBA Loan: $900,000 (90%) - Seller Note: $75,000 (standby for 2 years) - Your Cash: $25,000 (2.5%) Result: 97.5% financing!
Insider Tip: SBA allows seller financing as equity injection IF it's on full standby (no payments) for 24 months. Most sellers don't know this!

Hack #3: The Pre-Approval Power Move

Get Pre-Approved BEFORE Finding Deal

Why this works:

  • Shows sellers you're serious
  • Speeds up closing (30 vs 60 days)
  • Gives negotiation leverage
  • Locks in your rate

Pre-approval requirements:

  • Personal financial statement
  • 3 years tax returns
  • Resume with experience
  • Target industry identified

Hack #6: The Projection Game

How to Show 1.25x Coverage

Adjustments banks accept:

  • Add back excessive owner salary
  • Add back one-time expenses
  • Add back depreciation
  • Project 5-10% revenue growth
  • Show cost savings you'll implement
Current EBITDA: $200,000 + Excess owner salary: $50,000 + One-time legal fees: $20,000 + Your salary replacement: -$60,000 = Adjusted EBITDA: $210,000 Debt Service: $150,000 Coverage Ratio: 1.4x ✓

The Application Optimization Process

Documents That Win Approvals

The Killer Business Plan Sections:

  1. Executive Summary: One page, hit all strengths
  2. Management Experience: Emphasize ANY relevant background
  3. Market Analysis: Show growth, not competition
  4. Day 1-100 Plan: Detailed transition strategy
  5. Financial Projections: Conservative with upside
  6. Risk Mitigation: Address concerns proactively

Hack #8: The Seller Standby Note

Structure That SBA Loves

Total Price: $1,500,000 Buyer Down: $75,000 (5%) SBA Loan: $1,200,000 (80%) Seller Note: $225,000 (15%) Seller Note Terms: - 5 years at 6% - 2 year full standby - Payments start year 3 - Subordinated to SBA Your cash: Just $75,000 for $1.5M business!

Speed Hacks for Faster Closing

30-Day Close Strategy

Week 1:

  • Submit complete package Day 1
  • All docs organized and indexed
  • Pre-call lender with heads up

Week 2:

  • Respond to requests same day
  • Push for credit committee date
  • Get conditional approval

Week 3:

  • Complete all conditions
  • Order appraisals/environmental
  • Finalize purchase agreement

Week 4:

  • Final underwriting
  • Closing docs prep
  • Wire funds and close

Advanced SBA Strategies

The "Buyer Benefit" Negotiation

Ask seller to pay for these through higher purchase price (SBA finances it):

  • Training period (up to $50k)
  • Non-compete agreement ($25-100k)
  • Transition consulting ($2-5k/month)
  • Customer retention bonus

Result: Less cash needed at closing!
