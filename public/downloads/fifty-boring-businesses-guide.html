The Boring Business Success Formula

What makes these businesses goldmines:

  1. Essential Services - People need them regardless of economy
  2. Local Market Protection - Can't be outsourced to cheaper locations
  3. Recurring Revenue Models - Monthly/quarterly/annual contracts
  4. Simple Operations - Can be learned in 30-90 days
  5. Asset Light or Asset Backed - Either low capital or sellable assets
  6. Fragmented Markets - No Amazon or Walmart to compete with
  7. SBA Financeable - Banks understand and will lend

How to Find These Businesses

1. Direct Outreach (Best Results)

  • Google Maps: Search business type + your city
  • Call/email everyone with <10 reviews
  • Message template: "Looking to acquire a [business type] in [city]. If timing and terms were right, would you consider selling?"
  • Expect 10-15% response rate

2. Industry Associations

  • Join local trade associations
  • Attend meetings (everyone knows who's thinking of selling)
  • Sponsor events = instant credibility

3. Intermediaries

  • Business brokers (especially for $500K-$2M deals)
  • Industry suppliers (they know everyone)
  • Commercial real estate brokers (businesses + real estate)
  • Local CPAs and attorneys

4. Online Marketplaces

  • BizBuySell (largest, but competitive)
  • BizQuest, BusinessesForSale
  • Industry-specific sites
  • Facebook groups for business owners

Valuation Quick Guide

Most boring businesses sell for 2-4x Seller's Discretionary Earnings (SDE)

Typical Multiples by Type:

  • Service businesses: 2.5-3.5x SDE
  • Retail (with property): 3-4x SDE
  • Manufacturing: 3-4.5x SDE
  • Recurring revenue models: 3.5-5x SDE
  • Professional services: 3-5x SDE

Red flag: If they're asking more than 5x SDE, walk away (unless it's a very special situation).
