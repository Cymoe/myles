## Day 5: Specialized Supply Chain Plays

**Subject: The $4M business hiding in a strip mall warehouse**

Drive past any industrial park and you'll see them:

Boring warehouses. No signs. Maybe a few trucks.

Inside one of them: A business worth millions that nobody's competing to buy.

Here's what most buyers don't understand:

Distribution isn't about moving boxes. It's about EXCLUSIVE RIGHTS.

Let me show you what I mean:

Last month, a student found a medical device distributor:
- Revenue: $4M
- EBITDA: $800K
- Secret weapon: Exclusive territory rights for 3 manufacturers

Those contracts mean:
- Every hospital in 4 states buys through them
- Manufacturers can't add competing distributors
- Customers can't buy direct
- 20-year relationships locked in

This isn't competing on price. It's a legal monopoly.

**Here's the specialized supply chain playbook:**

**AVOID:**
- Commodity products
- Multi-line distributors
- Price-competitive markets

**TARGET:**
- Exclusive territories
- Technical products requiring expertise
- Regulated industries
- Long sales cycles

**The magic questions to ask:**

1. "What percentage of revenue comes from exclusive lines?"
2. "How long have you had these contracts?"
3. "When do they renew?"
4. "What's required to maintain them?"

**Red flag:** If they say "relationships" keep customers, run.
**Green flag:** If they show you contracts, lean in.

**Where to find these businesses:**

**METHOD 1:** Industry association directories
- Search: "[Industry] distributors association [state]"
- Look for member directories
- Target 10-50 employee companies

**METHOD 2:** Manufacturer websites
- Check their "where to buy" pages
- Note exclusive territories
- Contact those distributors directly

**METHOD 3:** Trade publication classifieds
- Industry mags always have "Business for Sale" sections
- Owners advertise where buyers aren't looking

Remember: These businesses are invisible by design. No websites. No marketing. Just contracts and cash flow.
