**Instead of:** "AS9100 certified aerospace manufacturer"

**Instead of:** "IDIQ contract vehicle"  
**Say:** "Pre-approved vendor status worth up to $10M in orders"

**Instead of:** "Proprietary ERP system"

**3. Progressive Draws:**
