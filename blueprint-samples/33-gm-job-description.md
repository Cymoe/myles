# General Manager Job Description Template
*[Customize for your specific service business]*

## The Opportunity

We're looking for an entrepreneurial General Manager to run our profitable [painting/cleaning/lawn care] business like it's their own. This isn't a job - it's a chance to earn what you're worth while building something meaningful.

**The Reality:** You'll manage a team of 3-10 people, serve 50-200 customers, and be responsible for $30k-$100k in monthly revenue. Your decisions directly impact your income.

**Your Earning Potential:**
- Base: $40,000-$60,000
- Performance Bonus: $20,000-$60,000+
- Total Realistic First Year: $60,000-$100,000
- Year 2+: $80,000-$150,000+

## What You'll Own

**Revenue Growth (40% of role)**
- Hit monthly revenue targets
- Upsell existing customers
- Maintain 95%+ customer retention
- Generate referrals systematically

**Operations Excellence (30% of role)**
- Schedule crews efficiently
- Maintain quality standards
- Handle customer issues fast
- Keep jobs profitable

**Team Leadership (20% of role)**
- Hire A-players only
- Train to our standards
- Build winning culture
- Fire fast when needed

**Financial Management (10% of role)**
- Weekly P&L ownership
- Control costs ruthlessly
- Report key metrics
- Project cash flow

## What We're Looking For

**Must-Haves:**
- Managed 5+ people successfully
- Comfortable with $500k+ P&L responsibility
- Track record of hitting targets
- Own reliable vehicle
- Live within 30 minutes

**Nice-to-Haves:**
- Service industry experience
- Built/scaled a team
- Sales background
- Systems thinker
- Bilingual (market dependent)

## What We Provide

**Tools for Success:**
- Proven business model
- Established customer base
- Marketing system
- Training program
- Operating capital

**Support Structure:**
- Weekly coaching calls
- Peer GM network
- Backend support team
- Growth resources
- Clear KPIs

## Compensation Structure

**Base Salary:** $40-60k (based on experience)

**Monthly Bonus Structure:**
- Hit revenue target: $1,000
- Exceed by 10%: $2,000
- Exceed by 20%: $3,500
- Maintain 95% retention: $500
- Zero complaints: $500

**Quarterly Bonus:**
- Profit sharing: 15% of profit above target

**Annual Bonus:**
- Business value increase: 5% of valuation gain
