# First Customer Scripts - Word for Word

## Cold Door Knock Script (Residential)

**Opening (Smile):**
"Hi, I'm [Name] with [Company]. I'm actually working with a few of your neighbors on [specific street] and noticed [specific observation about their property]. 

I have about 20 minutes before my next appointment - would you like me to give you a quick free quote while I'm here?"

**If Yes:** 
"Great! Let me take a 2-minute walk around and I'll give you an exact price. No pressure - if you like it, we can schedule. If not, no worries."

**If No/Not Interested:**
"No problem at all! Here's my card in case you need anything in the future. Who do you normally use for [service]?"

**Price Presentation:**
"Based on what I'm seeing, I can do this for $[price]. That includes [list everything]. I have an opening [specific day] or [other day]. Which works better for you?"

**Overcoming "Too Expensive":**
"I understand price is important. My price includes [list premium elements]. Plus, I guarantee [specific guarantee]. Most of my customers find they save money in the long run because they don't have to call me back to fix things. Would you like to start with just [smaller portion] to see the quality?"

## Commercial Cold Call Script

**Opening:**
"Hi [Name], I'm [Your name] with [Company]. I specialize in helping [type of business] save money on [service] while actually improving quality. 

I'm calling because I've been working with [similar business in area] and helped them cut their [service] costs by 20% while getting better results.

Do you have 2 minutes to discuss your current [service] situation?"

**If Yes - Discovery Questions:**
1. "Who handles your [service] currently?"
2. "What are you paying monthly/per service?"
3. "What's your biggest frustration with them?"
4. "If you could wave a magic wand, what would ideal [service] look like?"

**Pitch:**
"Based on what you've told me, I can:
- [Specific solution to their pain]
- [Cost saving or value add]
- [Unique differentiator]

I'd love to stop by for 10 minutes this week to show you exactly what I mean. Are mornings or afternoons typically better?"

## Referral Request Script (After Good Service)

**Setup:**
"[Customer name], I really appreciate your business. As a small local company, referrals from customers like you are how I grow. 

I'm wondering - who do you know that might need reliable [service]?"

**If They Can't Think of Anyone:**
"No worries! How about this - I'll leave you a few business cards. If you hear anyone complaining about their [service] or looking for someone reliable, would you mind passing one along?"

**If They Give Names:**
"That's fantastic, thank you! Would you prefer to introduce me, or would it be okay if I mentioned that you referred me?"

**The Golden Follow-up:**
"By the way, I give a $50 credit for every referral that becomes a customer. There's no limit - some of my customers have earned hundreds in credits!"

## Social Media DM Script

**Opening:**
"Hey [Name], I noticed you're in [neighborhood/area]. I'm actually doing several [service] projects there this week and have a small opening on [day]. 

Just wanted to reach out in case you needed any [service] done while I'm already in the area with my equipment. 

No worries if not - just trying to help neighbors while I'm there! 👍"

**If They Respond Positively:**
"Awesome! I can swing by for a quick free estimate either [time] or [time]. Takes about 5 minutes. Which works better?"

## Email Script for B2B

**Subject:** Quick question about [Company's] [service] costs

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

I'll keep this brief since I know you're busy.

I specialize in helping [city] businesses reduce their [service] costs by 20-30% while improving quality. 

I recently helped [similar company] save $[amount] per month by [specific improvement].

Worth a quick 5-minute call to see if I could do the same for you?

If interested, here's my calendar: [link]
If not, no worries - just trying to help local businesses save money.

Best,
[Name]
[Phone]

P.S. Even if you're happy with your current provider, I'll share the three questions you should ask them to potentially lower your current bill.

## The Money Line (Use in Any Script)

"Look, I know you get pitched all the time. Here's why I'm different: I guarantee [specific measurable result] or you don't pay. I'm that confident in what I do."

## Key Principles
1. **Always lead with value**, not your needs
2. **Be specific** - vague promises are suspicious
3. **Create urgency** without being pushy
4. **Ask for the sale** - most don't
5. **Follow up** - money is in the follow-up

*Note: Track your numbers. Expect 1 yes for every 10 conversations. That's normal. Volume wins.*
