# Home Service Business Selector Matrix

## Quick Decision Framework

### Your Starting Capital:
- **Under $1k**: Cleaning, Pressure Washing, Window Cleaning
- **$1k-$5k**: Painting, Lawn Care, Handyman
- **$5k-$15k**: Pest Control, Pool Service, HVAC
- **$15k-$50k**: Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing
- **$50k+**: Buy existing business instead

## The Winner's Formula

**Best Overall for Beginners**: Painting
- Low barriers to entry
- High margins (65-75%)
- Easy to systematize
- Year-round demand
- Simple to sell

**Fastest to $30k/month**: Cleaning (Commercial)
- Recurring contracts
- Minimal equipment
- Easy to hire for
- Predictable revenue
- Location flexible

**Best Exit Multiple**: Pool Service or Pest Control
- Highly recurring revenue
- Route-based efficiency
- Sellable customer lists
- Technical barriers = less competition
- PE roll-up targets
