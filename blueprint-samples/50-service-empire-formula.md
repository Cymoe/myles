### Business #1: The Learner ($500K-$1M Revenue)

**Target Profile:**
- Commercial cleaning or lawn care
- $100-200K EBITDA
- Owner wants out yesterday
- 5+ years operating history
- B2B focus (not residential)

**Financing Structure:**
- SBA 7(a): 80% ($400-800K)
- Seller Note: 15% ($75-150K)
- Your "Investment": 5% ($25-50K)
- Plot twist: Use ROBS or investor money for your 5%

**Negotiation Script:**
"Bob, I respect what you've built here. But we both know without you, this business is worth maybe 2x EBITDA. With you staying 6 months to train me? Different story. Help me succeed, and I'll give you 3x with terms that work for both of us."

**First 100 Days:**
- Fire bottom 20% of customers (unprofitable)
- Raise prices 15-25% (blame "new ownership")
- Systematize everything owner did manually
- Install GM from your pipeline (see Doc #33)
